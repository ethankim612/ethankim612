## About

I'm interested in how data can replace guesswork in everyday decisions, so that people regret fewer of them. That started with my school's daily logistics. I joined CSIALife as a co-developer with senior students and now lead its development, including a rebuild of the study hall request system that teachers once handled by hand. Since then I have taken the same approach into statistics research at CSIA R&D and into RESQON, a fire-rescue drone startup I co-founded.

## Research

As Founder & Lead Engineer of CSIA R&D, I run projects that use real school and public data.

- **Demand for new school services.** Modeled seminar room usage and site promotion engagement for new CSIALife services and applied off-policy evaluation. School research Bronze Award.
- **Korean grammatical change (NLP).** POS-tagged 1,391 sentences from 4 Korean literary works with Python and KoNLPy, compared shared and text-specific particles and endings in code, and wrote a paper.
- **Monte Carlo and real options.** Simulated binomial revenue paths for a small business to estimate survival probability and the optimal exit month. [Repo](https://github.com/ethankim612/PricingModel)
- **Gene expression time series.** Applied FFT to expression data for about 8,000 genes.
- **Sorting algorithm performance.** Measured bubble, quick, and merge sort across input sizes and initial orderings and compared them statistically.
- **Housing prices.** Cleaned King County sales data, engineered features, and compared linear regression with a random forest.
- **Meal preferences.** Used text embeddings and cosine similarity to relate students' meal preferences to menu items.
- **Small language models** *(in progress).* Building a small LLM and exploring diffusion-based LLMs.
